## Tanzania's least-known marine park

Mafia Island sits roughly 200 km south of Zanzibar and is a 30–50 minute flight from Dar es Salaam, and it gets a fraction of the visitors either of those places do. There's no bridge to fame here the way Zanzibar has Stone Town or the Serengeti has the migration — Mafia's entire pitch is one channel of water and what swims through it.

From October through March, whale sharks (*Rhincodon typus*) aggregate in the Mafia Channel in numbers the Marine Megafauna Foundation has tracked closely: about 208 individual whale sharks have been photo-identified off Tanzania over eight years of monitoring, with the population trend described as stable. That's a small population by global whale shark standards — the species itself has declined more than 50% worldwide over the last 75 years and is listed Endangered on the IUCN Red List — which is exactly why the rules around swimming with them matter as much as the sighting itself.

## When to go

| | |
|---|---|
| Best overall window | October–March |
| Peak whale shark sightings | November–December (through January) |
| Best diving visibility | October–March, 15–25m, ~29°C water Nov–Mar |
| Avoid | April–May (masika long rains; some lodges close) |

The dry season, June through October, brings the calmest seas and clearest skies generally, but whale shark season specifically runs October through March with peak reliability in November and December — scientific monitoring has recorded occasional sightings as early as June and as late as February, so the two calendars overlap rather than match exactly. October is the one month that scores well on both the general dry-season conditions and the whale shark calendar, which is part of why it's considered the strongest all-round month to visit.

## Getting there

There's no ferry service to Mafia Island — flying is the only practical option. Coastal Aviation, Auric Air and Flightlink run daily scheduled flights from Dar es Salaam's Julius Nyerere International Airport (DAR) to Mafia (MFA, also known as Kilindoni Airport), a roughly 30–50 minute hop, typically priced around $150 one-way if booked in advance.

Once you land, the airport sits near Kilindoni, but most lodges and the primary diving and whale-shark base are in Utende on Chole Bay, about 15km away — usually a lodge-arranged transfer or taxi, taking roughly half an hour. ## The whale sharks, and how the numbers work

Mafia's whale sharks are predominantly young males, which is a different demographic picture from some other global aggregation sites and one reason researchers keep monitoring it closely. Tourism operators advertise an 80–90% sighting success rate during peak season, though no operator can guarantee a sighting on any single outing. Outside the October–February window, sightings drop off meaningfully — this is not a year-round activity.

The Code of Conduct enforced within Mafia Island Marine Park bans touching, riding, blocking a shark's path, flash photography, overcrowding, and aggressive boat chasing. Swimming rules (group size, boat approach distance) are set by the marine park and your registered operator; ask them for the current code of conduct. The Whale Sharks Tour Operators Network of Tanzania (WATONET), supported by WWF, works on sustainable standards, and the NGO Sea Sense runs training workshops for boat captains and crews. Enforcement isn't uniform across every operator, though — a 2017 report noted that many sharks in the area already carry scars from boat strikes and propellers, so the operator you choose matters more here than almost anywhere else on this coast.

## Diving and snorkelling beyond the whale sharks

It's easy to let the whale sharks eclipse everything else Mafia offers, but Mafia Island Marine Park (MIMP) — gazetted in April 1995 and governed by Tanzania's Marine Parks and Reserves Unit (MPRU) — has a rich fish and coral fauna across its reefs. The same October–March window that's best for whale sharks is also the strongest diving window generally: calm seas, 15–25m visibility, and water around 29°C from November through March. If your trip doesn't line up with whale shark season, or you simply want more time in the water, the reef diving here stands on its own.

## Marine park fees, in practice

Mafia Island Marine Park charges non-resident adults USD 23.60 per person per day (USD 20 plus 18% VAT), with children about half (USD 11.80, ages 3–15), as quoted by Mafia lodges such as Butiama and Pole Pole; it is paid by credit card at the park gate, and the Marine Parks and Reserves Unit's own tariff page was not reachable when we checked, so confirm the current rate with your operator. Check with your operator whether any separate permit applies to a whale shark trip or a dive. Fees change, so confirm before booking rather than budgeting from an old blog post — ours included.

## Entry requirements and health

Mafia Island sits under mainland Tanzania's entry rules. Most EU and UK passport holders can get a single-entry e-visa for around $50, valid up to 90 days; US citizens need a $100 multiple-entry visa valid for a year, and the U.S. Embassy specifically recommends applying in advance rather than relying on arrival processing. A yellow fever vaccination certificate is mandatory if you're arriving from, or transiting 12 or more hours through, a country with yellow fever risk — the vaccine needs to be administered at least 10 days before entry. Malaria risk is moderate to high across Tanzania below 1,800m, including Mafia, and higher between November and May, so prophylaxis is strongly recommended for a trip timed to whale shark season.

## What this means for planning your trip

Book October through March if the whale sharks are the point, and lean toward November–December specifically for the best odds — but treat any operator's sighting guarantee with suspicion, since no one controls where the sharks are on a given day. Budget for the marine park fee as a real line item and ask your operator whether any separate permit applies, and choose your operator on how visibly they enforce the distance rules, not just on price.
