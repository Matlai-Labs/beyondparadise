Key figures at a glance

  • 206 whale sharks documented at Mafia Island, up from about 100 in 2012

  • 502,917–533,137 wildebeest counted by Oxford satellite survey in August 2023, against a long-standing estimate of 1.3 to 1.5 million

How many wildebeest take part in the Great Migration?

Between 324,000 and 1.5 million, depending on the method. The long-standing aerial-survey estimate is 1.3 to 1.5 million; a 2025 Oxford satellite study counted far fewer.

How many whale sharks visit Mafia Island?

The documented Mafia Island whale shark population more than doubled from about 100 in 2012 to 206 by December 2019, and 72% of research trips in the 2012-13 season saw at least one.

How many tourists visit Tanzania and Kenya each year?

This page does not yet publish national tourist-arrival or tourism-revenue totals. We list a figure only when two independent sources confirm it, and arrivals and revenue are not in our confirmed database yet. Until they are, use the national statistics offices directly: Tanzania's National Bureau of Statistics, the Zanzibar Office of the Chief Government Statistician, and Kenya's Tourism Research Institute.

Methodology and refresh schedule

  • Inclusion rule: a statistic appears only if its fact has status "confirmed" in our database, meaning two or more independent sources. Single-source figures are excluded.
  • Check dates: per-row dates run from 27 June 2026 to 8 October 2026. A row is only as fresh as its own check; we do not show a single page-wide date.
  • Refresh: reviewed quarterly. The page, the CSV and the dataset metadata are regenerated from the fact database at every site build, so a re-verified fact updates all three at once.
  • Disputed numbers: where sources disagree, as with the Serengeti wildebeest population, both figures are shown with their methods.
  • Citing: please cite as "Beyond Paradise Adventures, East Africa Tourism Statistics, 8 October 2026" and link to this page. Data download: CSV.
  • Independence: no operator or lodge pays for inclusion. See the editorial policy.
