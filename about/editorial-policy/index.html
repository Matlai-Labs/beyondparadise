Editorial Policy

The value of this site depends entirely on you being able to trust it. Here is exactly how we maintain that trust.

The non-negotiables

All stays are paid

Tim Hennig personally pays for every lodge stay reviewed on Beyond Paradise Adventures. Kim personally pays for every Vietnam property reviewed. We do not accept complimentary stays, hosted visits, or sponsored trips — from any operator, under any circumstances.

No press trips, ever

Press trips exist to influence coverage. A lodge curates what you see on a press trip — the best room, the best food, the attentive manager who shadows you all week. Tim pays to stay as a regular guest, with no lodge knowing in advance that a review is coming.

No sponsored content

Beyond Paradise Adventures does not publish sponsored content, native advertising, or paid placements. If you see a lodge reviewed here, it is because Tim or Kim stayed there and formed an honest opinion — not because the lodge paid for inclusion.

No Tim Score without a stay

The Tim Score is never assigned from secondary research, other people's reviews, or online sources. The number exists only because Tim was there, paying, forming a direct opinion.

Conflicts of interest are disclosed

Tim Hennig owns and manages Boutique Hotel Matlai in Zanzibar (Q140307008 on Wikidata). This is an undisclosable conflict of interest — Matlai will never appear in Beyond Paradise Adventures, positive or negative. If Tim has any other relationship with a property he reviews, that relationship is disclosed in the review.

Affiliate revenue

Beyond Paradise Adventures earns affiliate commission from some operators, booking platforms, and tour operators linked on this site (GetYourGuide, Viator, Stay22, and others). This never influences editorial decisions — a lodge is reviewed because Tim stayed there, not because an affiliate programme exists. All affiliate relationships are disclosed on relevant pages with a "We earn commission" note.

Fact sourcing standards

Every specific claim on this site — prices, seasons, sighting probabilities, conservation statistics — must be traceable to at least two independent sources, stored in our internal fact database. We maintain a tiered source registry (IUCN, government authorities, peer-reviewed journals, NGO research, operator data, and Tim/Kim firsthand observation). We publish the source tier with every critical fact.

Wildlife encounter probabilities and prices are volatile. We re-verify sighting data monthly during peak seasons and quarterly off-season. Prices are verified at time of writing and noted with a check date. Do not rely on price data more than 12 months old without checking the operator directly.

Ethics grading

Beyond Paradise Adventures grades wildlife operators on observable, documented criteria only: permit status, documented tourism-impact studies, verifiable conservation contributions, and habitat restoration records. We never assign an ethics grade based on marketing claims, sustainability certifications, or operator self-reporting without corroboration. When in doubt, the ethics grade is not assigned.

Corrections policy

If you believe a fact on this site is incorrect, email tim@beyondparadiseadventures.com with your source. If you are right, we correct the page immediately, add a correction note, and thank you. We are not embarrassed by corrections — we are embarrassed by uncorrected errors.
