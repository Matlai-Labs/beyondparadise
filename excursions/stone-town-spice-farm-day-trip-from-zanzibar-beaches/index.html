Stone Town & Spice Farm Day Trip from Zanzibar's Beaches

By Tim Hennig  ·  Updated 2026-11-15

The Short Answer

From Paje, Jambiani, Nungwi or Kendwa, the recorded road transfer to Zanzibar's airport side of Stone Town is 1 to 1.5 hours (50-60 km); from Michamvi, Boutique Hotel Matlai publishes about 1 hour 15 minutes by road direct to Stone Town. The spice farm visit itself takes about 3 hours (Zanzibar Tourism Board), so plan most of a day.

The airport is a further 5-6 km from Stone Town, about 8-15 minutes by taxi, typically $5-15 (Zanzibar.com and Visit Zanzibar Island, checked 30 June 2026).

What the day is

A Stone Town and spice farm day combines two things. Stone Town (Zanzibar City) was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2000 for its Swahili, Arab and Indian merchant architecture, carved wooden doors and its past as an Omani Sultanate capital and Indian Ocean trade hub. The farm visit covers the cloves, nutmeg, cinnamon, pepper and vanilla still grown on Unguja, the main island, which in the 19th century was the world’s largest clove producer and earned the nickname Spice Island. Spice farm tours from Stone Town take approximately 3 hours (Zanzibar Tourism Board).

How long the road takes from each area

The answer for four of the five areas is the same recorded range: resort-to-airport transfers to Kendwa, Nungwi, Paje and Jambiani are about 50-60 km and take roughly 1 to 1.5 hours by road (Tuliza Zanzibar and Zuri Zanzibar, checked 30 June 2026). Michamvi has its own figure, to Stone Town itself rather than the airport: about 1 hour 15 minutes by road, as published by Boutique Hotel Matlai in Michamvi Pingwe on its fact sheet (checked 15 November 2026). That is a hotel’s own published number, not a measurement by us. Nungwi and Kendwa are 3-4 km apart, roughly a 9-minute drive (Destinationz, DistancesFrom.com, checked 23 September 2026), so those two start points are practically one.

Two honest limits. These are transfer ranges, not measured door-to-door times, and traffic into Stone Town in the morning is not recorded here. Ask your lodge for the pickup time and the expected return time before you book.

Paje and Jambiani, east coast south

Paje has two kitesurfing seasons: Kaskazi from mid-December to February or March, with north winds averaging 15-18 knots, and Kuzi from roughly May to October, with south-to-southeast winds of 15-25 knots. March, April, May, October and November are the off-season (KiteActive, B4Kite, checked 30 June 2026). If wind decides your days, a calm day is the one to spend in Stone Town. Jambiani, just south of Paje, is one of the beaches where green sea turtle nesting is recorded, generally February to July (Zanzibar Ecotourism, Sea Turtle Status, checked 30 June 2026). See sea turtles in Zanzibar for how to watch them without harming them.

Michamvi, southeast peninsula

Michamvi Pingwe is a small fishing village on the southeast coast, with calm lagoon water on the west side and open Indian Ocean on the east. It is the only area where we cite a direct drive time to Stone Town, about 1 hour 15 minutes by road, from Boutique Hotel Matlai’s published fact sheet. See the Zanzibar travel guide for the seasons that apply to the whole island.

Nungwi and Kendwa, north coast

Nungwi is the built-up north tip. It holds the Mnarani Marine Turtles Conservation Pond, which reported 13 green turtles and 4 hawksbills in September 2005 (Zanzibar Ecotourism, checked 30 June 2026), a count that is old, so ask before you plan around it. Kendwa stays swimmable through the tide cycle thanks to a deep shelf close offshore, while the sea at Nungwi’s tip can recede tens to hundreds of metres at low tide (Altezza Travel, Destinationz, checked 23 September 2026). That matters for your beach days, not for this trip.

What we do not know yet

We have no measured door-to-door drive time for Paje, Jambiani, Nungwi or Kendwa, no named spice farm, no entry fee and no price. Those would need a real trip with the date and time measured, and we will not guess. Until then, your lodge is the right source for the current operator, price and pickup window.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the drive to Stone Town from the beach areas?

For Kendwa, Nungwi, Paje and Jambiani we record one shared figure: about 50-60 km and 1 to 1.5 hours by road to the airport, which is a further 5-6 km (about 8-15 minutes by taxi) from Stone Town. For Michamvi we cite the figure published by Boutique Hotel Matlai in Michamvi Pingwe: about 1 hour 15 minutes by road direct to Stone Town (hotelmatlai.com fact sheet, checked 15 November 2026). We have no measured door-to-door time for the other four areas, so ask your lodge for today's figure.

How long does a spice farm visit take?

Spice farm tours run from Stone Town take approximately 3 hours, according to the Zanzibar Tourism Board. Add the road time in both directions and a Stone Town walk and the whole outing fills most of a day.

Which pickup area is best for this trip?

None is clearly closer in our records. Nungwi and Kendwa are only 3-4 km apart (about a 9-minute drive), so their transfer is effectively the same. Choose your base for its beach, not for this trip; the day trip works from all five.

Can I do this from Paje on a day without wind?

Yes. Paje is Zanzibar's kitesurfing hub, and the sources we use list March, April, May, October and November as the kitesurf off-season, which makes a low-wind day a natural time for Stone Town.

Do you sell or run this tour?

No. Beyond Paradise Adventures is an independent editorial site and does not resell day tours. Ask your lodge to confirm the current operator, pickup time and price.
