Safari Blue Dhow Trip, Menai Bay: Fumba from Stone Town

By Tim Hennig  ·  Updated 2026-09-23

The Short Answer

Safari Blue Dhow Day Trip, Menai Bay from Stone Town / Zanzibar Airport: ~20min drive from Stone Town direct to Fumba. Lunch is served on Kwale Island, a small protected island inside the Menai Bay Conservation Area reached from Fumba Beach by dhow — the same waters where one Zanzibar tour agency (Eco Culture) reports dolphin sightings on roughly 85% of Safari Blue trips (a single, unaudited source; never guaranteed on any single day).

What Safari Blue Dhow Day Trip, Menai Bay actually is

Safari Blue is a full-day traditional dhow trip through the Menai Bay Conservation Area, a 470 km2 marine protected area off Zanzibar’s south-west coast that has been gazetted since August 1997. The dhow departs from Fumba village, about a 20-minute drive from Stone Town, and the day typically includes a sandbank stop, reef snorkelling inside the conservation area, a lookout for Indo-Pacific bottlenose and humpback dolphins, and a Swahili seafood lunch on Kwale Island.

Getting there from Stone Town / Zanzibar Airport

Safari Blue’s own pickup point is Fumba village, roughly a 20-minute drive south-west of Stone Town. Our source data treats Stone Town and Zanzibar Airport as a single starting point for this transfer (they’re only 5-6km apart), so budget roughly half an hour door-to-dhow from either.

A note on this figure: We don’t have a fact-checked direct transfer time from other beach areas (Paje, Nungwi, Michamvi, Jambiani, Kendwa) to Fumba specifically — only this Stone Town/airport figure. Ask your lodge to confirm road time from further afield.
