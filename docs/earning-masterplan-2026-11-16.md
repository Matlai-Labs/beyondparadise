# Beyond Paradise Adventures: how the brand can earn

Status: draft plan of 16 Nov 2026, written by the assistant after three audit rounds and one independent review, not yet approved by the owner.

Labels: **confirmed** means read on the programme's or platform's own page on 16 Nov 2026. **Estimate** means a keyword tool's monthly average. **Weak evidence** means one blogger, one vendor or a small sample. **Assumption** means my own estimate. The working steps, sources and evidence are in the separate task list.

## 1. The verdict in five lines

1. Today there is nothing to sell. Nobody counts the readers, there is no way to ask for help, no legal pages, and no first-hand material on the site.
2. The brand can own one thing nobody else has: a daily, dated record of what the sea, the beach and the rules on Zanzibar were really like, with photos and gaps shown.
3. That record is the proof inside the question travellers ask most: "what is this month really like, and on which coast?" A fee must never be able to buy a verdict.
4. The first money step is free: readers ask, the brand introduces an operator it would recommend anyway, nobody pays. Fees come later, and only after gates, a legal opinion and published rules.
5. Year 1 income: about zero. Year 1 builds one asset: the record, and the month pages built on it.

## 2. The unfair advantage

**The sentence.** Beyond Paradise Adventures is the Zanzibar travel publication that writes down every day what the sea, the beach and the rules were really like, from people on the island, shows the record with its gaps and photos, and says "skip this" when that is true.

**The product.** The honest answer to "what is this month really like, and on which coast", with the record as its proof. Tide and seaweed notes are proof inside "when to go". They are not pages of their own. The demand says why (estimates from a keyword file, Google Ads monthly averages, 1 Nov 2026, 1,482 keywords in 7 languages, picked from Zanzibar topics, so they lean toward them):

| Search topic | Searches a month (estimate) |
|---|---|
| Best time to visit, weather, rainy season (all languages) | about 22,000 to 28,500, depending on which keywords are counted |
| "zanzibar quando andare" (Italian, one keyword) | 9,900 |
| "sansibar beste reisezeit" (German, one keyword) | 6,600 |
| Is Zanzibar safe (all languages) | about 6,300 to 6,400 |
| Tides (all languages) | about 550 to 800 |

| "zanzibar quando non andare" (Italian, "when not to go") | 260 |

**Why it is hard to copy, and where it is not.**
- A rival with money can hire an observer within weeks. That part is not hard.
- A rival cannot go back in time. Every day recorded is a day nobody else has.
- A seller cannot say "skip this week". It earns from selling that beach and that trip.
- A writer abroad cannot fill in the past. A funded media house finds the market too small to try.
- So "unfair" means: too honest for sellers, too daily for bloggers, too small for big publishers. It is a head start plus an incentive sellers do not have. It is not a wall.

**What exists today, said plainly.** A daily log started on 7 Nov 2026. Most of its rows are model figures filled in backwards, not observations. No observation by a person is saved yet. The form to record one exists and is tested. **Until 25 of 30 days are observed by a person, this is a plan, not an advantage.**

**The day-30 proof.** It passes when all three hold:
1. At least 25 of the first 30 days have an observation by a person with a photo from the fixed point.
2. On at least a fifth of the observed days, the observation differs from the model in a way a traveller would act on (swim possible, sandbank visible, amount of washed-up seaweed).
3. No bad day was softened, and at least one "skip" or "think twice" line went out unchanged.

If it fails, the plan falls back to the earlier version: a small independent guide with a free enquiry test, and decision 1 decides whether to fold it into the owner's other site.

## 4. The five decisions

### Decision 1: what the brand is, and which site holds the record

**Recommended:** "Zanzibar from the ground, with proof." Same brand name, a new line under it: "Recorded on the island, dated, checked."

| | Today | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| Reader | Couples spending over 6,000 euros on East Africa lodges | Anyone choosing when and where to go on Zanzibar |
| Product | Scored lodge reviews | The record, the month answers, "skip" verdicts, an official-rules tracker |
| Money | Commission on luxury trips | Free matching first; a fee later only after gates |

**What the owner gives up:** scored lodge reviews across East Africa, and "trips above 6,000 euros only" as the definition of the reader. **What he keeps:** the name, the independence promise, the channels, and the 6,000-euro trip as the kind of trip behind the free enquiry: safari plus Zanzibar.

**Which site holds the record.** The owner's other site, WildToSea, is larger, has English and German pages and own photos, gets very little search traffic from Google today, and covers the same subject. It also names the owner's hotel on many pages. Recommended split:
- WildToSea: the broad Tanzania and Zanzibar library in English and German. It links each month to this brand's month page.
- Beyond Paradise Adventures: the record, the month pages, the "skip" verdicts, the rules tracker, the free enquiry.
- The hotel's own site: no tide or seaweed page of its own. It may link to the record and say it is the owner's.

The reason: a record of the owner's beach on a site that promotes his hotel reads as marketing. On the brand with the stricter promise it reads as evidence. The counter-argument is strong: the other site has more readers. Two tests settle it: a side-by-side comparison of both sites over the same 28 days, and, after the first month page, five links to it from the other site, then 60 days of Bing and AI-assistant citations compared.

**If the owner does not decide:** the record is kept privately and nothing from it is published.

### Decision 2: legal entity, one legal opinion, and the data licence

Who publishes, signs and is paid, and in which country? This decides imprint, privacy page, taxes, payouts and which YouTube programmes are open (Tanzania and Germany differ, confirmed).

One written legal opinion must answer, before any fee:
1. When the brand introduces a reader to an operator outside the EU, who is the organiser under the EU package travel rules? The directive gives an EU retailer the organiser's duties when the organiser is outside the European Economic Area, unless the retailer shows the organiser meets them (Article 20, confirmed).
2. Do agent licensing and ownership rules in Tanzania and Zanzibar apply to someone who passes enquiries for a fee?
3. What consent wording and privacy page are needed when an enquiry goes to an operator in Tanzania?

The opinion costs money (assumption: a few hundred euros). It is the owner's approval.

**The data licence.** The weather and tide model figures in the log come from a free service whose terms say: only for non-commercial use (confirmed). Three options: a paid licence; another source; or the public record shows only human observations and photos. **Recommended:** the public record shows human observations and photos only. The model figures stay internal, for checking, until a paid licence or another source is approved.

**If the owner does not decide:** no imprint, no privacy page, no enquiry form, no model figures in public.

### Decision 3: the owner's hotel, and bad days

Two rules together:
- **No scored stay reviews and no stay referrals in Zanzibar.** Zanzibar coverage is the sea, the beaches, seasons, rules, excursions and transfers.
- **Bad days are published.** Every observation goes out as written and is never softened. That includes the beach next to the owner's hotel.

An older Instagram post that shows a villa of the owner's hotel gets a one-line note ("Our own family hotel. Not a review.") or is removed.

**If the owner cannot accept the bad-days rule, the advantage does not exist.** The plan then falls back to the earlier version.

### Decision 4: who records

A named role and a backup among the people on the island. About five minutes a day. One fixed photo point, the same frame every day, no people in it. A weekly check by the assistant. The owner gives this task to his own staff. The assistant never messages staff.

**If the owner does not decide:** nothing is recorded, and the advantage cannot start.

### Decision 5: a standing approval for record posts

Posts made only from the record (the daily or weekly observation with its photo) may go out without a separate yes. Everything else still needs his yes per post.

**If the owner does not decide:** every record post waits for his yes. Past evidence: most drafts expired that way.

**The fee question** is no longer a decision now. It comes at the day-90 gate of the free enquiry test.

## 5. How the brand will earn

**Main line: free matching first.** The enquiry form sits on the month pages and in the letter. The trip type is safari plus Zanzibar. A reader asks; with written consent, the owner introduces one or two operators the brand would recommend anyway. Nobody pays the brand. The 90 days start on the day the first month page goes live. Fewer than 3 real enquiries: the referral line waits. 3 or more: the owner decides whether a fee is acceptable at all. A fee starts only after that gate, one written legal opinion, and the published rules. One 9,000 euro trip at a 5 percent fee would pay 450 euros.

**Supporting links (the owner asked for affiliate income).** No paid link before the site has 1,000 measured visits a month and the data licence is settled. The one exception: the untrue sentence in the editorial policy is corrected now. Only where a link fits the record, never on a page that judges the same excursion, always labelled at the top:

| Line | Programme and rate (confirmed) |
|---|---|
| Day tours a reader asked about | Viator 8 percent; GetYourGuide at least 8 percent; Civitatis 8 to 10 percent |
| Airport and hotel transfers | Welcome Pickups 8 percent (network listing, not confirmed on its own page) |
| Evacuation cover | Global Rescue 10 percent |
| Travel insurance | World Nomads, Heymondo (rate not published) |
| eSIM | Airalo up to 10 percent |

**Later options.** A fixed-fee planning call, only after the legal opinion and the free test. A paid market brief for the trade belongs to the owner's intelligence project, not here: the owner manages a hotel that competes in that market.

**Rejected.** Display ads (little money, cheap look). Host travel agency (the brand would sell what it judges). Sponsored content, paid placement, a paid operator directory (breaks the promise). Machine-written pages in bulk (Google counts it as spam, confirmed). Paid traffic. Hosted group trips (the organiser is liable for every service). Selling follower reach (nothing to sell). Selling the record's data (no buyers found). A paid conditions alert (no demand evidence).

## 6. The independence rules, ready to publish

Title: "How Beyond Paradise Adventures earns, and what money can never buy".

1. No lodge, operator or platform can pay for a review, a ranking, a mention, or to be left out. Ever.
2. We list a partner only after we have published an assessment of it against criteria that are also published.
3. We introduce readers only to operators we would recommend with no fee.
4. In our own written agreements every partner pays the same fee. We do not mix ready-made programmes that pay different rates.
5. Zanzibar hotel nights are never part of a fee.
6. An operator that does business with the owner's hotel is named as such on this page, or we do not use it.
7. You pay the same price whether you come through us or go direct. Each agreement says so in writing.
8. We take no hosted stays, free trips, press trips or gifts.
9. Partners never see, approve or change a text before it is published.
10. Every page with a paid link says so at the top.
11. This page lists every current partner and the fee.
12. We never review, score, rank or refer to the owner's hotel.
13. We publish no display ads, no sponsored posts and no paid placements.
14. We publish bad days. A saved observation is never edited or deleted. Corrections are added with a date.
15. The record describes the sea and the place, never the owner's hotel. The method page says who records it and how that person is tied to the owner's hotel.

Fixed notes on every record page: "Not a safety forecast." and "Seaweed farming exists only in Paje, Jambiani and parts of Bwejuu. Washed-up seaweed and sea conditions in this record are observations of the beach and the water."

**Editorial policy, money paragraph, now:** "Today Beyond Paradise Adventures earns no money from this site. No page carries a paid link, and no lodge, operator or platform pays us."

**Footer:** "No paid reviews. No hosted stays."

## 7. What each channel is for

The record feeds everything, in this order:
1. **Observe** (daily, the recording role, five minutes): photo from the fixed point plus a short form.
2. **Join** (nightly, automatic): each row marked "observed" or "model"; differences flagged.
3. **Check** (weekly, assistant): photo against form; gaps stay gaps; seaweed wording checked.
4. **Weekly note** (standing approval): three lines plus "go / think twice / skip", one short clip or photo post, the Facebook mirror.
5. **Month page**: "Zanzibar in [month]: what it was really like", with days recorded, swim and sandbank windows, washed-up seaweed days, rain days, rule changes, one verdict, and a download of the data.
6. **Citable data**: one stable name for the record, so Bing and AI assistants can cite it.
7. **Weekly letter**, after the privacy page: the note plus the latest month page and the free enquiry.

## 10. How it is measured

Free tools: a visit counter without cookies, Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, a monthly check of which AI assistants cite the record, YouTube and Instagram statistics, a private enquiry list.

**The monthly one-page review:**
1. Days recorded out of days in the month; days where observation and model differ.
2. Visits, Bing impressions of the month pages, AI-assistant citations of the record.
3. Enquiries: received, introduced, booked.
4. Instagram and YouTube: reach, link taps, clicks to the site.
5. Corrections made and re-checks failed.
6. Cash and hours spent.
7. Which gate is closest; one decision for the owner.

Approval prompts must reach the owner; tested monthly.

## 11. What to stop

1. Scored lodge reviews across East Africa leave the plan. The scoring page says plainly that no scored reviews are planned. The home-page counter of zero scores goes.
2. "Trips above 6,000 euros only" as the definition of the reader.
3. The unsupported lines in the brand's rules: every claim first-hand, and a count of properties reviewed.
4. Any line that says where the owner lives. Use "Recorded on Zanzibar every day."
5. The Vietnam hub and new Asia work (see section 7).
6. The marine encounter index as the "keystone product". It may return as one part of the record if a partner supplies real counts.
7. Tourism statistics posts on Instagram and Facebook.
8. Any operator recommendation or price taken from the owner's hotel's suppliers.

## 12. What would make this fail

- Nobody records. The weakest link is one person writing it down every day while the owner is away.
- The owner softens a bad day. Then the record is a brochure.
- A reader takes a tide note as a safety forecast.
- People change and the role has no backup. A gap in the record is permanent.
- Spending on design, stays or more pages before readers exist.
- A partner bends a verdict or charges the reader more.
- Two sites keep competing for the same searches and the same hours.
