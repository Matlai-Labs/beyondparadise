# Templated excursion pages: similarity review (2026-10-09)

Analysis only. No page was changed. Context: Google's 24 Sep 2026 spam update targets sets of near-identical, templated pages.

## Method
Built the site (`astro build`), took the visible text inside `<main>` of each built page (scripts/styles stripped, lower-cased, punctuation removed), and compared word shingles.
- J5 = Jaccard similarity of 5-word shingles (the strict "same sentences" measure).
- J3 = Jaccard of 3-word shingles.
- C5 = containment: share of the shorter page's 5-word shingles that also appear in the other page.
Main text includes the shared page chrome inside `<main>` (quick-facts table headings, related-excursion block), so numbers are slightly high; the pages are generated by `pipeline/seo/generate-excursion-pages.js` from one template.

Reference point (unrelated excursion pages, different template content): J5 0.11 to 0.13, C5 0.20 to 0.25.

## What the overlap is
- The "What the trip actually is" section (UNESCO Stone Town 2000, spice history) is identical on all five spice pages.
- The transfer section uses the same "50-60 km, 1 to 1.5 hours, plus 8-15 min airport to Stone Town" figures for every area. That is a single airport-transfer figure reused for five different places, which both reads as templated and is probably not accurate per area (Nungwi in the far north is not the same drive as Michamvi or Jambiani).
- Only the "What <area> brings" paragraph differs (Paje: kitesurf seasons; Nungwi: north-coast character and the Mnarani turtle pond; and so on), plus the title and meta description.
- Each page carries a "No direct X-to-Stone-Town drive time is recorded" note, which is an honest disclosure but also signals thin, data-free content.

## Risk assessment
J5 0.38 to 0.53 across a 5-page set that differs mainly by a place name and one paragraph is the classic "doorway/template set" shape. The pages also compete for the same query ("Stone Town spice tour"), splitting relevance. Mnemba pair (0.35) is lower risk but shows the same pattern. The other excursion pages are not affected (J5 about 0.12).

## Recommendation
1. Spice tour (5 pages): consolidate to ONE page, "Stone Town and spice farm day trip from Zanzibar's beaches", with a short section per pickup area (Paje, Jambiani, Michamvi, Nungwi, Kendwa: drive time, best day, what suits that area). Use a neutral new slug and 301 all five old URLs to it. Remove the five from the sitemap in the same release.
2. Mnemba (2 pages): keep both only if each gets genuinely different first-hand content (departure point, boat, timing, what you saw). If Tim cannot supply that, consolidate the same way into one page with two "departing from" sections.
3. Do not rely on rewording by the generator. Paraphrased template text keeps the same structure and does not fix a doorway-page pattern; only unique facts do.
4. Order: consolidate first (fast, removes the risk), then add first-hand content to the surviving page.

Option "keep as is" is not recommended while the spam update is rolling out. Option "add unique first-hand content to all 7" is the best long-term result but depends entirely on the facts below.

## Facts Tim would need to supply (per pickup area, per trip he has actually done)
Spice tour, for each of Paje, Jambiani, Michamvi, Nungwi, Kendwa:
- Real door-to-door drive time to Stone Town by private car and by dala-dala, with the date and time of day measured. Typical private-transfer price.
- Best departure time from that area and the time you are back (so readers can plan around lunch/sunset/flights).
- Which spice farm he used (name, location, entry fee, whether a guide is included), how long the visit lasted, what was tasted/bought.
- Anything specific to leaving from that area (road condition, ferry or traffic bottlenecks, tide/kite-wind timing for Paje, early-morning start for Nungwi).
- One or two of his own photos from the day, with captions.

Mnemba, for the Kendwa and the Stone Town airport departures:
- Departure point, boat type, who runs it, price paid, group size, duration on the water.
- Marine conservation area fee (amount and who collects it) and whether it is included.
- What he saw (species, visibility), the month, the tide, and one thing that went wrong or surprised him.
- Whether he would book the same operator again (the site's ethics grade depends on this).

Without these, consolidation is the only honest option.
